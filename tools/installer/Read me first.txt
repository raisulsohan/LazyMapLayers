============================================================
  LAZYMAPLAYERS 0.2
  Free map animation for After Effects: globe to street level,
  3D terrain, looks, labels, routes, highlights, rendered
  frame-exact
============================================================

WHAT YOU NEED
  - Windows 10 / 11, or macOS
    (tested on Windows 11; the macOS installer has not been
    tried on a Mac yet)
  - Adobe After Effects 2024 or newer
  - A graphics card with WebGL 2 (any card from the last
    ten years)


------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALL
------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Unzip this download anywhere. The Desktop is fine.
  2. Close After Effects if it is open.
  3. Windows: double-click  "Install LazyMapLayers.bat"
     macOS:   double-click  "Install LazyMapLayers (macOS).command"
              (if macOS refuses, right-click it and choose Open)
  4. Wait for "Installed", then press a key.
  5. Start After Effects and open:
       Window > Extensions > LazyMapLayers

  The panel is signed, so nothing else needs installing and
  no Adobe setting has to be changed.


------------------------------------------------------------
YOUR FIRST MAP IN A MINUTE
------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Open the maps list (the button at the top left) and click
     "Build the world flight sample". It builds a 36-second
     scene: the globe turns while borders draw on, the camera
     flies to Paris, then along a route to Tokyo, with labels,
     pins and callouts.
  2. Click "Render". The map is rendered for every frame and
     imported into the map comp.
  3. Press play (or render with After Effects as usual).

  For the street-level ending, move the preview over Paris,
  click the download button (arrow, top right), name it  paris ,
  and do the same for Tokyo ( tokyo ). Then build the sample
  again.


------------------------------------------------------------
MAKING YOUR OWN MAP
------------------------------------------------------------
  Maps list        (top left) your maps, "New map", the sample.
  New map          adds a map comp to the comp you have open, at
                   its size (or to a new scene).
  Preview          the exact frame: drag to move, scroll to
                   zoom, right-drag to rotate and tilt.
  Basemap          World (offline) or downloaded regions.
  Globe            a planet at low zoom that becomes the flat
                   map by zoom 8.
  Search           a country, province, district, city or
                   "lat, lng", offline, in 26 languages.

  CAMERA
  Shots tab        "+ Shot" takes the view in the preview. Two
                   shots make a move: Fly, Straight, Along route
                   or Cut, with a duration and an easing. "Play"
                   runs it in the preview. "Apply to timeline"
                   writes the keys (one undo).
  Keyframe view    keys the preview's view at the current time.
  Fly here         keys one smooth flight from the camera at the
                   current time to the preview.
  Live link        while on, moving the preview moves the map at
                   the current time.
  3D camera        adds an After Effects camera that matches the
                   map, so 3D layers sit on the ground.

  TOOLS (the row of icons)
  Pin, 3D pin      click, then click a place (or Alt+click the
                   map; Alt+Shift+click for a 3D pin).
  Callout          a leader line with a title box next to a place.
  Route            a great-circle line between two places.
  Highlight        click countries, provinces or districts on the
                   map; each renders as its own layer above the
                   basemap. Districts are downloaded per country
                   when you first ask for them.
  Import           GPX, KML, KMZ, GeoJSON, CSV or a zipped
                   shapefile: draw lines as routes (with an arrow
                   that rides along, at the recorded pace of a
                   GPS track if you like), pin places, highlight
                   areas, or fly the camera along a line.
  Auto labels      country and city names in the local language,
                   as editable text layers that never overlap or
                   flicker. Pick how many.
  Animate borders  draws the borders on over 4 seconds.
  Look             Midnight, Satellite, Daylight, Atlas,
                   Blueprint, Mono, Paper; shaded relief; the sky
                   above the horizon; terrain (below).

  RENDER
  Preview          half resolution, fast.
  Render           full quality. Only frames that changed are
                   drawn again.
  Render tab       supersampling, motion blur, passes and mattes
                   for this map.

  Every panel action is one Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z).


------------------------------------------------------------
TERRAIN
------------------------------------------------------------
  In the Look sheet, "Terrain > Download..." fetches an elevation
  pack for the area in the preview (open elevation data through
  Mapterhorn; the size is shown first). With a pack:
    Shaded slopes  sharp hillshading at any zoom.
    3D height      real mountains, 1x true to scale or more.
                   Pins, labels and routes made with the pack sit
                   on the ground. Key the map layer's "Terrain
                   Height" slider and the mountains rise.


------------------------------------------------------------
MAP DATA AND DOWNLOADS
------------------------------------------------------------
  The world map (Natural Earth) and the provinces of every
  country are inside the panel and work offline. Everything
  else is downloaded only when you ask, and the size is shown
  first:
    - OpenStreetMap detail for an area (the download button):
      roads, buildings, water, from the free Protomaps planet
      build.
    - Satellite pictures (NASA Blue Marble, 20 MB) and shaded
      relief (Natural Earth, 48 MB): Look sheet.
    - District boundaries per country (geoBoundaries, open
      data): Highlight tool > Districts.
    - Elevation packs (Mapterhorn, open data): Look sheet.
  Downloads are saved in
    Windows  %APPDATA%\LazyMapLayers
    macOS    ~/Library/Application Support/LazyMapLayers

  Credits: rendering an OpenStreetMap region, district
  boundaries or terrain adds one small credit text layer to
  the scene ("(c) OpenStreetMap contributors", "Boundaries:
  geoBoundaries", "Terrain: (c) Mapterhorn"). Keep it, or put
  the credit in your video's end titles.


------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------
  The panel is not in the Window menu
      Restart After Effects. Adobe only looks for new panels
      while the app is starting.

  The panel opens blank
      Windows: run "Fix a blank panel.bat" and restart After
      Effects.
      macOS: in Terminal run
        defaults write com.adobe.CSXS.12 PlayerDebugMode 1
      and restart After Effects.

  The map preview stays black
      Update the graphics driver. The panel needs WebGL 2.

  Renders take disk space
      Renders of a saved project go to a "LazyMapLayers
      Renders" folder next to the project. You can delete that
      folder when the project is finished.


------------------------------------------------------------
UNINSTALL
------------------------------------------------------------
  Windows: double-click  "Uninstall LazyMapLayers.bat"
  macOS:   double-click  "Uninstall LazyMapLayers (macOS).command"
  Both ask before deleting downloaded data.
