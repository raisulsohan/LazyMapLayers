Pick LazyMapLayers when

  • the budget is zero and nothing may expire
  • you work without internet, or want street detail anywhere with no subscription
  • you need a globe, one smooth flight to street level, and crisp frames at every zoom
  • you want 3D terrain and compositing passes without extra plugins
  • you tell data stories (flows, prisms) or history (borders by year)

Pick GEOlayers 3 when

  • you work on a Mac, or on After Effects older than 2024
  • you want Esri or Bing imagery built in
  • you want paid support and a long track record

How this was put together

Written by the author of LazyMapLayers, so read it with that in mind. The GEOlayers 3 rows come from the aescripts product page, MapTiler's guide, the aescripts forum and the help that ships with GEOlayers 3 up to v1.19. Later versions may have changed things, and today's price is on the aescripts page. If a row is wrong, please open an issue on the LazyMapLayers GitHub and it will be corrected.
